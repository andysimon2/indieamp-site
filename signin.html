Sign in

IndieAmp runs on your network’s own address.

Each client organization has its own private web address, so there is no shared sign-in here. Find your way in below.

Member stores

Your personal link

Your network sent you a personal link to your store portal, and your weekly report email carries it too. Open it on a phone or a desktop; nothing to install.

Prefer to sign in with your email? Go to your network’s address and add /login; a sign-in link is emailed to you. Lost both? Ask your network’s contact or send us a message.

Network staff

Your organization’s dashboard

Sign in at your network’s address followed by /internal/login with your work email; a single-use link is emailed to you. Your organization controls who is on the team.

Brand partners

Your brand page

Use the private link your network gave you, or sign in with your email at your network’s address followed by /b/login.

Not sure which address your network uses? Send us a message with your network’s name and we will point you to it.

Not a member yet?

IndieAmp is offered through associations, co-ops and buying groups to their member stores. If your network is not on it yet, we are happy to talk to them.
