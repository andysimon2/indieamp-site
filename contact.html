Member stores

Your network sends you a personal link to your portal, and your weekly report email carries it too. If you have lost it, ask your network’s contact or write to hello@indieamp.app with your store’s name and your network.

To disconnect an account, use the Disconnect button on your account page, or remove IndieAmp in TikTok’s or Facebook’s own app settings.

Brand partners

Your network gives you access to your brand page. Questions about the numbers on it go to your network first; questions about the service come to us.
