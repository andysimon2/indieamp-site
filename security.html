Security & privacy

Data & privacy, in plain English.

We read the minimum needed to do the job, with consent, through official channels. The full detail is in our Privacy Policy — this page is the honest summary, and how the service is built to keep it true.

What we access, and why

Four kinds of data, each with a reason.

WHAT WE ACCESS WHY
Public profile basics — account name, handle, follower count To identify the connected account and show network membership accurately
Content the member posted — captions, dates, links, formats To attribute activity to campaigns and brands, and surface top posts
Engagement metrics — likes, comments, shares, views, reach To measure performance for the member and the network
Publishing permissions (only if the member opts in) To post content the member has explicitly chosen and scheduled

What we never do

  • No passwords. Authorization is OAuth-only, on the platforms' own screens, revocable by the member at any time.
  • No selling data. A member's data serves their own network's reporting — nothing else.
  • No cross-client sharing. Each client organization's data lives in its own isolated database and is never visible to another client.
  • No posting without consent. Nothing is ever published to a member's accounts unless that member explicitly scheduled or approved it.
  • No consumer tracking. We measure business accounts' content performance — we do not profile or target consumers.

How we protect it

Built so one client’s data stays that client’s.

One isolated database per client

Each client organization runs on its own database and its own web address. A request is tied to the organization it arrived for, and there is no shared pool of member data across clients.

Access tokens encrypted at rest

The platform tokens members grant are stored encrypted, with a separate key per client. They are decrypted only at the moment a read or a publish is made on the member’s behalf.

Official APIs, least privilege

Every read and every publish goes through TikTok’s and Meta’s official APIs with the permissions each platform has reviewed and approved for IndieAmp. Publishing permissions are requested only when a network uses publishing.

Revocation that takes effect

A member can disconnect from the account page or from the platform itself. Posts queued for a disconnected account are cancelled, and a token the platform reports as invalid is flagged for the member and the network at once.

Staff access by named sign-in

Network staff and brand contacts sign in with single-use email links. Access is per organization, can be removed by the organization, and ends immediately when it is.

Backups, encrypted

Each client’s database is backed up nightly to encrypted storage, with the decryption key held offline, so a mistake can be undone without the backup itself becoming a copy of the data anyone can read.

The documents

The Privacy Policy sets out what is collected, why, how long it is kept and how to ask for it to be deleted. The Terms of Use govern the service for networks, members and brand partners. Questions about either go to hello@indieamp.app.
