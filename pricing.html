Pricing

Priced per network, by member count.

One subscription covers the organization, every member store and every brand partner. No per-seat fees, no per-post fees, no charge for members to connect.

Because every network is a different size and runs a different program, we quote each one. The quote is a flat fee set by the number of member stores, billed monthly or annually, and it includes everything below.

What is included

Everything, for everyone in the network.

The organization

Your own environment

  • A private, isolated database and your own web address
  • Network dashboard and brand reporting for any period
  • Campaign library, programs, suggested calendar, download folders and inspiration cards
  • Unlimited staff sign-ins
  • Nightly encrypted backups
Member stores

Every store, every platform

  • TikTok, Instagram and Facebook connections for every member
  • The store portal on phone and desktop, with several people per store
  • Self-service, approve-each-post and Hands-free publishing
  • The weekly performance email
  • Quick Start guides for stores and for your staff
Brand partners

Brand pages, unlimited

  • A page for every sponsoring brand, with sign-in for the brand’s own people
  • Downloadable reports
  • Share of voice and format mix across the network

Onboarding

Included. We import your member roster from a spreadsheet, create every store’s personal link, set up your programs and brands, and send the first invitations with you. The first stores can be connecting within days.

Support

Included. Email support for your staff, your members and your brand partners, with a reply within one business day. Members’ reconnection and account questions come to us, not to you.

Is there a free trial?

We run a live demo with sample data for your team first, and most networks start with a pilot group of stores before rolling out to every member. Ask us about a pilot when you request a demo.

Do member stores pay anything?

No. The network’s subscription covers every member. Stores never see a bill from IndieAmp.

Do brand partners pay anything?

Not to IndieAmp. How a network funds its program with its brand partners is the network’s own arrangement.

How is the subscription billed?

Monthly, or annually at a discount. Either way it is one subscription for the whole network, under a written service agreement.
