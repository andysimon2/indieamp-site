Built among independents

Real roots. Shared ambition.

IndieAmp grew out of the day-to-day work of social measurement and brand reporting for independent retailers.

Where it came from

Independent retailers are good at what they do in their stores. Social media is one more job on top of that, and the brands they carry rarely get to see what the stores do for them online, because it happens one account at a time across dozens of towns.

IndieAmp started as the reporting behind a national network of independent specialty retailers: reading members’ TikTok, Instagram and Facebook activity with their consent, adding it up by brand, and showing the brands behind the network what the whole network does. Publishing followed, because the easiest way to get members posting is to hand them something ready to post and let them keep control of it.

What we believe

  • Members keep control. Every post is the store’s own, on its own accounts, visible to the store before it goes out, and cancellable until then.
  • Consent is the product. Connections run on the platforms’ own screens, can be revoked at any time, and publishing is something a member turns on, never something done to them.
  • Honest numbers. One engagement formula everywhere, the same number in the member’s email, on the network dashboard and on the brand page.
  • One client, one database. A network’s data serves that network. It is not pooled, sold or shown to anyone else.
