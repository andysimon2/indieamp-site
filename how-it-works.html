How it works

A simple connection. A bigger picture.

A network joins, its members connect their own accounts through the platforms’ own screens, and the activity adds up into reports for everyone involved.

Step by step

From sign-up to the first report.

01

Your network joins

Your organization gets its own private, isolated environment with its own database and its own web address. Your staff sign in by email link. You add your member stores from a spreadsheet or one at a time, and each store gets a personal link to its portal.

02

Members connect

A store opens its link and connects TikTok, Instagram or Facebook. Authorization happens on TikTok’s and Meta’s official login and consent screens. IndieAmp never sees a password. A store can connect one platform, two or all three, and disconnect any of them later.

03

Members choose how to post

Post themselves from the content library, approve each post the network schedules for them, or turn on Hands-free so dated posts are queued to their calendar each week. Every post is visible in the store’s own calendar before it goes out, where it can be edited or cancelled.

04

The picture comes to life

Posts and their engagement are read from the connected accounts through the platforms’ official APIs. Each store gets a weekly email about its own week; the network sees the whole picture on its dashboard; brand partners see their brand’s page.

A typical week

What happens once it is running.

For a member store

New campaign content appears in the library. Posts the store scheduled, or approved, go out at their times in the store’s own time zone. A weekly email arrives with the store’s numbers, its best post and the network’s leaders.

For the organization

Staff upload the week’s content and date it on the suggested calendar, see which stores shared each campaign, check store health (connections that need attention, accounts that went quiet) and read the network’s totals for any period.

For a brand partner

The brand page updates as members post. Posts that mention the brand are counted toward it automatically, so a brand sees its activity across the network without anyone tagging it by hand.

Common questions

Answers before you ask.

Do members give IndieAmp their passwords?

No. Connection is OAuth only, on TikTok’s and Meta’s own screens. IndieAmp receives an access token the member can revoke at any time, both from IndieAmp’s account page and from the platform’s own settings.

Which accounts can connect?

A TikTok account, an Instagram professional account (Business or Creator) linked to a Facebook Page, and a Facebook Page the member administers. Personal Instagram profiles without a linked Page cannot be read through Meta’s API.

Can anything be posted without the member knowing?

No. Every scheduled post appears in the member’s calendar first, where it can be edited, moved or cancelled. Hands-free posting is something the member turns on, and on TikTok each post still needs the member’s own tap and visibility choice.

Who can see a store’s data?

The store itself, the staff of its own network, and, in aggregate only, that network’s brand partners. Each client organization has its own isolated database; no other client can see it. Nothing is sold or shared outside the network.

What does a member need to install?

Nothing. The store portal is a web page that works on a phone or a desktop, reached by the store’s personal link or by signing in with an email address.

How does a store disconnect?

From the Disconnect button on its account page, or by removing IndieAmp in the platform’s own app settings. Posts still queued for that account are cancelled.
