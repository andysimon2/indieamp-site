The platform

Social reporting and publishing for member networks.

One service, three audiences: the organization that runs the network, the independent stores that belong to it, and the brands whose products they sell.

IndieAmp is a B2B software platform for membership organizations of independent retailers: buying groups, associations and co-ops. Member stores connect their TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook Page accounts via secure OAuth. IndieAmp reports on how their posts perform, gives them ready-to-post campaign content from their network, and adds the whole network’s activity up into reports for the organization and its brand partners.

What each part of the network gets

Built for all three sides of a member network.

01 / Associations & buying groups

One clear view of the member network.

  • A network dashboard: posts, engagement, connected stores, brand performance, store health, top posts and best posting times across every connected account.
  • A campaign library: upload photos and videos with captions for each platform, crop per channel, add the brand logo, and publish the campaign to every member at once.
  • Programs: named groups of stores, with content that only participants can use when a campaign calls for it.
  • A suggested calendar: date each post for members, and optionally have it posted for the stores that choose Hands-free posting.
  • Download-only folders for files members take rather than post, and inspiration cards for ideas members act on themselves.
  • Who shared what: per-campaign counts by channel, and the stores that have not yet posted.
02 / Member stores

Make posting the easy part.

  • A weekly performance email: posts, engagement, views and reach for the week, with the store’s best post and its place in the network.
  • A store portal, reached by a personal link, with the network’s content library, a calendar of what is queued and what posted, a week planner, and an account page.
  • Scheduling to TikTok, Instagram and Facebook from a phone or a desktop: pick the channels, edit the caption, choose the time, and the post goes out at that time in the store’s own time zone.
  • Per-post control: edit or cancel anything before it publishes, crop the image per platform, add the store’s own logo, add a link to a Facebook carousel.
  • Several people per store, with an Admin and Staff role, each signing in by email link.
  • Disconnect any account at any time; nothing queued for it publishes afterwards.
03 / Brand partners

A clearer view of a brand across the network.

  • A brand page for each sponsoring brand: posts, engagement, share of voice, format mix, top posts and a monthly trend for the period the brand picks.
  • Network context: how the brand’s activity compares with the network as a whole, for the same period.
  • A downloadable report (CSV) with the same numbers.
  • Aggregate only: brand pages show the network’s totals and top posts, never individual stores’ private details.
  • Sign-in by email link for the brand’s own people, or a private link from the network.
Publishing, the member’s way

Three ways a store can post.

Each store chooses, and can change its mind at any time on its account page.

Post it yourself

The store opens a campaign in its library, picks the channels and a time, and schedules it. The post is the store’s own, on its own accounts, under its own name.

Approve each post

The network schedules a campaign for the store. The store gets a notice before it goes out and can edit, move or cancel it from its calendar. Nothing publishes without the store having had the chance to say no.

Hands-free

The store turns Hands-free on itself. Posts the network has dated are then queued to the store’s calendar for the coming week, and a weekly email lists them. On TikTok every post still waits for the store’s own tap and its own visibility choice, as TikTok requires.

Brand-sponsored campaigns carry each platform’s branded-content disclosure. A store can turn Hands-free off at any time; anything it queued is cancelled.

Data we access

The minimum needed to do the job.

  • Member stores’ own TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook Page accounts (with OAuth consent).
  • Public post data — captions, timestamps, permalinks, likes, comments, shares.
  • Instagram Insights (reach and Reel plays) per post, via the connected Page.
  • Basic profile data — display name, follower count, linked Instagram Business account.
  • Scheduled publishing — with a store’s explicit authorization, IndieAmp uploads and publishes store-approved campaign posts to the store’s own connected accounts. Every scheduled post is visible in the store’s portal first, where the store can edit, reschedule, or cancel it; brand-sponsored posts carry the platform’s branded-content disclosure.

We do not store consumer or follower-level personal data, send messages on stores’ behalf, or access private direct messages. Each store can revoke access at any time through their platform settings.
